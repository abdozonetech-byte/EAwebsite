AI & Business Consultant in Morocco: Start with a Measurable Use Case

An AI and business consultant helps a company start with a concrete operational problem, choose a realistic use case, organise data and review steps, and build a measurable pilot. The aim is not to add AI everywhere.

By Elboubakry Abdessamad · Marketing & applied AI consultant, Casablanca

Who is this approach for?

SMEs, service companies, marketing teams, schools, clinics, professional practices, shops and digital projects can examine AI opportunities without losing control of their processes.

Based in Casablanca, I work on site or remotely according to the engagement. The diagnostic starts with existing operations: acquisition, enquiry qualification, CRM follow-up, content, reporting, documentation and internal coordination.

Useful AI and business needs

  • Strategy: identify problems where AI could create observable value before choosing tools.
  • Assistants and agents: prepare research, summaries or classifications within a defined scope, with sources and review requirements.
  • Process automation: connect forms, email, CRM, notifications, documents and reporting to reduce repeated entry.
  • Marketing support: structure research, briefs, content, qualification and analysis while retaining human decisions.

Connect marketing, AI and CRM

A business may attract prospects through search, social platforms or content, then lose value through weak qualification or follow-up. AI can support the existing journey by summarising a need, classifying an enquiry, preparing a CRM entry or suggesting a follow-up for review.

Keep the original lead source, authorised data, human decisions and action history. Connecting a tool does not grant permission to collect restricted data or send messages automatically. Customer contact and important commitments require appropriate controls.

Review the process before automating it

Keep a human approval step when an action affects a customer, payment, sensitive information or a significant commitment. Choose the smallest useful scope rather than maximum autonomy.

  • Leads: reception, qualification, assignment, notification and follow-up.
  • Marketing: research, briefs, variants, calendars, quality checks and reporting.
  • Customer service: request triage, draft answers and escalation to a person.
  • Documents: extraction, summaries, classification and retrieval from authorised sources.
  • Management: data consolidation, alerts and preparation of useful indicators.

Diagnose, prioritise, pilot and measure

  • Diagnostic: map the process, tools, available data, risks and cost of current friction.
  • Prioritisation: select one bounded use case based on value, feasibility, risk and measurability.
  • Pilot: build a testable workflow with access rules, expected outputs and human reviews.
  • Measurement: compare time, quality, errors and effort before deciding to improve, expand or stop.

Deliverables and limits

Depending on the agreed scope, an engagement may produce a process map, prioritised use cases, assistant prototype, automated workflow, API integration, CRM connection, documentation and a measurement plan. My work connects digital marketing, project management, AI-assisted development, APIs, Supabase and CRM.

AI does not guarantee automatic growth, zero errors or full autonomy. Useful systems need suitable data, clear instructions, tests, human oversight and ongoing review. Sometimes conventional automation or better organisation is sufficient.

Questions & answers

How can a business start an AI project with controlled risk?

Begin with a short diagnostic and one priority use case, with a defined scope, authorised data, human reviews and clear success criteria.

Can AI, marketing and CRM work together?

Yes. A scoped integration can connect forms, qualification, CRM, notifications, draft follow-ups and reporting without replacing sales decisions or human relationships.

Is support available outside Casablanca?

I am based in Casablanca and can support projects elsewhere in Morocco or remotely depending on the engagement.
