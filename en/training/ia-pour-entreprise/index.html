AI for Business

Choose an operational problem and build a proportionate solution. Use AI to prepare, organise and check work while keeping a person responsible for decisions.

What you will be able to do

  • Prioritise an AI use case with concrete criteria
  • Prepare productivity and marketing workflows
  • Design a website or CRM around a real need
  • Organise human review and data protection
  • Present a testable business project

The programme, module by module

01AI for entrepreneurs

Capabilities, limits, total cost and use-case selection.

Practical exercise
Compare three tasks and choose a pilot with an observable outcome.

02Productivity & prompts

Context, instructions, examples, verification and document work.

Practical exercise
Create a summary workflow with sources and a list of missing information.

03Build a website with AI tools

Offer, content, pages, mobile, forms and generated-code review.

Practical exercise
Prepare a website prototype and test the clarity of its call to action.

04CRM & business automation

Pipeline, authorised data, triggers, stages and exceptions.

Practical exercise
Map enquiry → qualification → next action with a manual recovery path.

05AI-assisted marketing

Research, briefs, message variants, calendars and reporting.

Practical exercise
Build a brief and connect each content item to a verifiable customer question.

06Responsible AI & practical project

Privacy, bias, errors, review, documentation and measurement.

Practical exercise
Present a pilot, its tests, when to stop it and decisions reserved for a person.

Your final project

A concrete, reviewable outcome.

A documented business pilot: process, prototype, evaluation criteria, responsibilities and improvement plan.
