From strategy to qualified enquiries

Digital Marketing & Acquisition

Build a coherent acquisition journey: a clear offer, suitable channels, a landing page and lead follow-up. Each module turns a marketing decision into a reviewable deliverable.

Enquire about training ↗WhatsApp ↗

What you will be able to do

  • Connect the offer, audience and objectives to a marketing plan
  • Prepare Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns with explicit hypotheses
  • Review a page for SEO, GEO and conversion
  • Define useful events, KPIs and CRM stages

The programme, module by module

01Foundations & strategy

Positioning, audience, customer journey and social media strategy.

Practical exercise
Write a one-page brief and a content calendar addressing three customer objections.

02Meta Ads & Google Ads

Objectives, intent, campaign structure, message, creative and measurement.

Practical exercise
Design two campaign plans and a test matrix without publishing or spending.

03SEO & GEO

Search intent, architecture, useful answers, sources and limits of generative visibility.

Practical exercise
Audit a page and propose titles, internal links and verifiable answers.

04Landing pages & conversion

Hierarchy, evidence, forms, accessibility and mobile journeys.

Practical exercise
Prototype a landing page and check its mobile journey.

05Analytics & KPIs

Google Analytics, Search Console, events, UTMs and data quality.

Practical exercise
Define a measurement plan distinguishing clicks, enquiries, qualified leads and customers.

06CRM & lead management

Qualification, stages, ownership, next actions and follow-up.

Practical exercise
Organise a test pipeline and handle a duplicate or incomplete enquiry.

Your final project

A concrete, reviewable outcome.

An acquisition portfolio: brief, campaign plans, landing page, measurement plan and demonstration CRM pipeline.
