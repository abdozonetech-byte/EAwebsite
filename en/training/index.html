Frequently asked questions

Which track should I choose?

Choose Digital Marketing & Acquisition for campaigns and lead follow-up; AI Development & Automation for coding and integrations; AI for Business for an operational use case without prior programming.

Are these programmes suitable for beginners?

Each programme lists its level and prerequisites. A discussion helps adapt the starting point to your experience and objective.

How do I request training?

Contact me by email or WhatsApp with your chosen track, goal and current level. Arrangements are confirmed directly before any commitment.

Do you also offer consulting and development?

Yes. Training complements marketing strategy, web and AI development, CRM and automation services. You can learn, commission implementation or discuss an appropriate combination.
