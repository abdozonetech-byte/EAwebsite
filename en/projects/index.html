Projects · Morocco

Projects designed as useful systems.

These demonstrations show my approach and the components I can build. They are not client case studies or evidence of sales performance, and they contain no invented client results.

01 · Demonstration

Lead system & CRM

An architecture connecting a prospect’s arrival, qualification, appointment request and sales follow-up in one structured view.

Components

  • Qualification form
  • Lead routing and statuses
  • Notes, appointments and history
  • Source and event tracking

Intended value

Avoid losing enquiries and make the next action visible for every contact.

02 · Demonstration

Conversion landing page

A page dedicated to one decision, organised around the message, objections, trust and a measurable call to action.

Components

  • Promise and editorial hierarchy
  • Evidence sections and answers to objections
  • Form or booking journey
  • Mobile performance and tracking

Intended value

Give traffic a coherent destination and reduce friction before contact.

03 · Demonstration

SEO, Search Console & analytics

A measurement foundation connecting search intent, pages, traffic sources and meaningful conversions.

Components

  • Content and semantic architecture
  • Structured data and internal links
  • Google Search Console and GA4
  • Events, UTMs and a tracking dashboard

Intended value

Understand what attracts a relevant audience and prioritise improvements using reliable data.

04 · Demonstration

AI & business automation

A functional demonstrator showing how an incoming enquiry can connect to AI-assisted qualification, Supabase, CRM, notifications and human review. It does not present invented client results.

Demonstrated journey

  • Capture and normalise a lead from a form
  • Prepare qualification against defined criteria
  • Store the record in Supabase and display it in CRM
  • Notification, status, next action and human review

Verifiable components

  • Responsive CRM interface and pipeline states
  • Data structure, APIs and error handling
  • Tracking events and enquiry source
  • Documented, controllable and reversible workflow

Transparent limits

Validate architecture and user experience, not a claim of client sales performance or autonomous AI decisions.
