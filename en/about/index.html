A systems-oriented approach.

My work combines digital marketing, applied business AI, web development, automation, project management and training. This business and technical perspective helps me translate a problem into clear requirements, user journeys, conversion pages, tracking and controlled workflows.

I start by understanding the offer, clarifying messages, choosing channels and preparing pages, then measure conversions and improve progressively. Strategy and implementation stay connected: from defining the need and coordinating the project to reviewing AI-assisted work and connecting it to SEO, campaigns, CRM and useful indicators.

Questions about my work.

What is your digital marketing approach?

I connect strategy, content, campaigns, conversion pages, analytics and automation into a coherent system. The aim is to make marketing clearer, measurable and useful for the business.

Do you work as a freelancer, consultant or marketing project manager?

An engagement can combine strategic advice, marketing planning, coordination, campaign monitoring and conversion-journey improvements, depending on the need.

What kinds of projects can you support?

The approach can suit SMEs, local services, schools, clinics, applications, digital projects, personal brands and businesses seeking to organise digital acquisition.

Which tools do you use?

Depending on the project, tools can include Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GA4, Meta Pixel, Google Search Console, OpenAI Codex, Claude, Cursor, GitHub, Supabase, Cloudflare, REST APIs, CRM, forms and automation.

What happens in the first consultation?

We discuss the project, objective, offer, audience, existing channels and obstacles, then clarify priority actions.
