Who is Elboubakry Abdessamad?

Elboubakry Abdessamad is a digital marketing and applied-AI consultant based in Casablanca, Morocco. His work connects strategy, acquisition, websites, CRM, analytics and controlled automation.

Where does Elboubakry Abdessamad work?

I am based in Casablanca and work with businesses and projects across Morocco, on site or remotely depending on the engagement.

What can you help with?

Digital strategy, campaigns, SEO, landing pages, analytics, CRM, AI assistants and process automation.

Which businesses do you work with?

Small and medium-sized businesses, local services, digital projects, schools, clinics, specialists and personal brands looking to organise acquisition or operations.

How do we choose where to start?

A diagnostic identifies the priority business problem, useful data, risks and next action before committing to a wider implementation.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or mentions in AI answers?

No. The work focuses on useful content, crawlability, accurate business information and evidence-based measurement. Search rankings, indexing and citations in AI-generated answers are not guaranteed.
