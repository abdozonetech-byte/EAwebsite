Design a neutral survey questionnaire

Write clear, single-focus questions with suitable answer options and an explicit unknown or not-applicable choice where needed.

01 — AI Prompt

Define your context.

Original prompt
Act as a critical research analyst.

Task: Design a neutral survey questionnaire.

Input context:
- RESEARCH GOAL: [RESEARCH GOAL]
- AUDIENCE: [AUDIENCE]
- CONSTRUCTS: [CONSTRUCTS]
- FORMAT: [FORMAT]

Write clear, single-focus questions with suitable answer options and an explicit unknown or not-applicable choice where needed. Check wording, order and respondent burden. Include a pilot plan and avoid inventing validated scales or representative sampling.

Never invent sources, citations or completed research. Inaccessible references remain unverified.

Use English for explanations unless the task or user explicitly requires another language. Use clear sections and a format suited to the deliverable. If essential information is missing, ask a focused question before concluding. Label assumptions and unknowns. Do not invent facts, sources, results or completed checks. End with verification criteria and remaining limitations.

02 — Example Result

A concrete example to review.

An editorial illustrative example, not a model-tested or guaranteed result. It does not change when you edit variables.

Questionnaire review

Replace “How much do you love this convenient service?” with a neutral question about actual use, followed by a separate satisfaction item when relevant. Do not force a positive or negative answer from non-users. Pilot comprehension before launch. No responses, validation evidence or representative research findings are created by this example.

What you get
