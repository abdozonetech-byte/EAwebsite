Appointment forecast plan

First compare a recent-average baseline with a seasonal baseline if enough history exists. Hold out a suitable period and explain how missing bookings are handled. Capacity and planned closures may limit useful demand forecasts. Without actual evaluation, the output remains a plan; no prediction interval, accuracy score or guaranteed volume is reported.
